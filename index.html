Folio V

About Remi Vance

I am a visual artist and writer who works slowly. After completing studies in traditional fine arts and comparative literature, I realized that my favorite work happened not in high-gloss white-cube galleries, but at a wooden table covered in tea rings, pressed leaves, and open notebooks.

My work is an investigation into stillness. I draw with materials that have their own memory: hand-mulled inks from walnut husks, charcoal burnt from willow stems, and cold-pressed rag papers that absorb water like dry soil. In my writing, I look for the same quiet honesty — capturing the weight of an hour, the blue of twilight, or the dignity of leaving a sketch intentionally unfinished.

Materials Kept in Daily Use
